Executives and senior professionals

When you leave a corporate role, you usually have 60 days to elect COBRA. Before you do, compare it with individual and private options: COBRA is often the most expensive way to keep the same network. See COBRA alternatives and health insurance for executives.

Executives coming off a corporate PPO usually want similar flexibility; individual and private PPOs are worth comparing with COBRA. Read our PPO guide.

Early retirees in the golf communities

Many residents of PGA National, BallenIsles and Mirasol retire before 65. We compare COBRA, Marketplace and private plans with your financial advisor or CPA, because expected income affects Marketplace pricing. See early retirement health insurance.

Severance packages and employer-paid COBRA

Executives leaving a company sometimes receive a severance package that includes several months of employer-paid COBRA. That can make COBRA the obvious choice for the subsidized months, and the question becomes what to do when the subsidy ends. Plan the next step before then: voluntarily dropping COBRA mid-year usually doesn't open a Marketplace Special Enrollment Period, but COBRA running out does, and open enrollment is always available. Private plans can be applied for year-round if you're healthy.

HSAs for high earners

An HSA-qualified high-deductible plan lets eligible people contribute pre-tax money to a Health Savings Account that can be invested and carried forward. For healthy households with the cash flow to handle a higher deductible, it can be a useful planning tool. Your CPA or financial advisor can confirm whether it fits your tax picture; we show which plans qualify.

Golf-community couples with different health needs

In PGA National, BallenIsles and Mirasol we often see one spouse who is healthy and one managing a condition. Spouses don't have to be on the same plan. One may choose a guaranteed-issue Marketplace plan and the other a private underwritten plan, if that lowers total cost without risking access to care.

Your Palm Beach Gardens game plan

  1. Read your severance terms for any COBRA subsidy and its end date.
  2. Mark your 60-day COBRA election deadline.
  3. Compare COBRA with individual, private and spouse-plan options.
  4. Decide whether an HSA-qualified plan fits (with your CPA).
  5. Plan the switch for when any subsidy ends.

FAQ

Palm Beach Gardens health insurance questions

How long do I have to elect COBRA?

Generally 60 days from the later of your coverage end date or the date of your COBRA notice. Compare your options before electing.

Is Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center in every plan?

Participation varies by carrier and plan and can change yearly. We confirm it before you enroll.

Can I buy a private plan right after leaving my job?

Healthy applicants can apply for a private underwritten plan at any time of year; approval is up to the carrier. Losing employer coverage also opens a Special Enrollment Period for Marketplace plans.

If my severance pays COBRA for six months, what then?

When the employer-paid period ends, you can stay on COBRA at full cost (within the usual time limit), choose a private plan if you're healthy, or move to a Marketplace plan at open enrollment or when COBRA runs out.

Can my spouse and I be on different plans?

Yes. Spouses can choose different plans if that works better for cost or access.

Is your consultation really free?

Yes. Brokers earn a commission from the carrier; it isn't added to your premium.
