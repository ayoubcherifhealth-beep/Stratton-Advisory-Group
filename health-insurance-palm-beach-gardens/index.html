FAQ

Palm Beach Gardens health insurance questions

How long do I have to elect COBRA?

Generally 60 days from the later of your coverage end date or the date of your COBRA notice. Compare your options before electing.

Is Palm Beach Gardens Medical Center in every plan?

No. Hospital and doctor networks differ by carrier and by plan, and can change each year. We check your specific doctors and hospitals against each plan's directory before you enroll.

Can I buy a private plan right after leaving my job?

Private underwritten plans are available year-round for healthy applicants, subject to approval. Losing employer coverage also opens a Special Enrollment Period for Marketplace plans.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. Our advice is free; carriers compensate brokers. You pay the same premium you would pay going to the carrier directly.
