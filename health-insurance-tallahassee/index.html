Leaving a State or University Job in Tallahassee? COBRA vs. Individual Coverage

Many Tallahassee professionals spend years in state government or at a university, then move into consulting, lobbying or private practice. When that employer coverage ends, you’ll be offered continuation coverage. It keeps your plan, but you pay the full premium plus up to 2%, and that bill can be a shock.

You generally have 60 days to elect COBRA, and coverage is retroactive, which gives you time to compare. We quote individual plans with PPO networks that include Tallahassee Memorial and your specific doctors, and show you the cost difference in dollars.

Retiring before 65? COBRA usually runs 18 months, which may not reach Medicare. We compare individual plans that can bridge the full gap, with a focus on a low max out-of-pocket. And if you’re now self-employed, your premiums may qualify for the self-employed health insurance deduction.

FAQ

Tallahassee health insurance questions

What happens to my health insurance when I leave a Florida state job?

You’ll typically be offered continuation coverage, where you pay the full premium plus up to 2%. You can also compare health insurance plans plans, which are often less expensive. We help you compare before your election window closes.

How do early retirees in Tallahassee get health insurance before Medicare?

Options include COBRA or other continuation coverage and individual plans. Since COBRA often lasts 18 months, many early retirees need a individual plan to reach age 65. We compare all of them.

Can Tallahassee consultants deduct health insurance premiums?

Self-employed consultants with a net profit may deduct premiums for themselves, a spouse and dependents if they weren’t eligible for an employer-subsidized plan that month. Confirm with your tax professional.

Is Tallahassee Memorial in-network?

It depends on the carrier and plan. We verify Tallahassee Memorial, HCA Florida Capital Hospital and your specific doctors before you enroll.

Do you serve Tallahassee remotely?

Yes. We serve Tallahassee and Leon County fully by phone and video.

I'm a graduate student with a family. What coverage options do we have?

Students may have school-sponsored coverage, and dependents may be covered under it or need their own plan. Depending on household income, marketplace tax credits or Florida KidCare for children may help. We compare options with you.

Do Tallahassee plans include specialists in Gainesville or Jacksonville?

Some do. If you rely on specialists in larger systems outside Leon County, we check how each plan covers them.
