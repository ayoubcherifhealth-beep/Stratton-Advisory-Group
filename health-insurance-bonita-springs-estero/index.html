Coverage until Medicare in Bonita Springs

The most common question we get here is how to stay covered between leaving work and turning 65. The usual options are COBRA, an ACA Marketplace plan and a private underwritten plan. COBRA keeps your network at full cost plus up to 2%. Marketplace pricing depends partly on household income, which can be lower in early retirement. Private plans can cost less for healthy applicants but may exclude pre-existing conditions. Our early retirement guide walks through each option.

Seasonal residents

If you spend part of the year in Michigan, Ohio, Illinois or the Northeast, a plan built around Lee County hospitals may only cover emergencies up north. We compare national PPO options against local plans based on where you actually receive care. More in our guide for seasonal residents.

Two counties, one daily life: Lee and Collier networks

Bonita Springs and Estero are in Lee County, but many residents use doctors in Collier County, just minutes south. Health plans are filed by county and networks are built regionally, so a plan priced for your Lee County address might include your Naples specialist, or might not. Before we compare prices, we list the doctors you see on both sides of the county line and check each one.

The same applies to hospitals. Some residents prefer Lee Health facilities, others NCH in Naples. A lower-cost plan that leaves out the system you actually use usually isn't a saving.

Your first year of retirement income

For many Bonita Springs couples, the first year after leaving work is the hardest to plan. Marketplace tax credits use your estimated household income for the coverage year, and that income often comes from a mix of severance, pension, investment income and withdrawals. If your estimate is too low, you may have to repay some of the credit at tax time; if it's too high, you may pay more than necessary during the year.

We build two or three income scenarios with you and your CPA and show how each changes the plans you're comparing. See early retirement health insurance in Florida.

New to Estero? A short checklist

  • Find out whether your current plan has any Florida network. Most out-of-state individual plans don't.
  • If you had coverage in the 60 days before your move, you likely have 60 days after it to enroll in a Marketplace plan.
  • Pick your Florida pediatrician and primary care doctor first, then choose a plan that includes them.
  • Keep proof of your new address and your prior coverage.

A five-step plan for Bonita Springs and Estero

  1. List doctors in both Lee and Collier counties.
  2. Estimate household income for the coverage year (with your CPA if you're retiring).
  3. Compare Marketplace, private and COBRA options on total yearly cost.

FAQ

Bonita Springs & Estero health insurance questions

Can I see doctors in both Naples and Fort Myers on one plan?

Sometimes. Some plans include providers in both Collier and Lee counties; others are narrower. We check the specific doctors you use in each county before recommending a plan.

I retired early and my income dropped. Does that change my Marketplace price?

It can. Premium tax credits on HealthCare.gov are based on estimated household income and size for the year, so a lower retirement income can change what an eligible household pays. We estimate it with you and your CPA.

Is Lee Health in every plan?

Lee Health is in many local plans but not every one, and lists change each year. We look up the exact plan before you enroll.

Do you serve Fort Myers and Cape Coral too?

Yes. We work with clients across Lee County by phone and video.

What if my income estimate for the Marketplace is wrong?

Premium tax credits are reconciled on your federal tax return. If your income ends up higher than estimated, you may repay some of the credit; if lower, you may get more back. Update your estimate during the year if things change.

Do Bonita Springs plans include Naples doctors?

Some do and some don't. Networks are regional and differ by plan. We check the specific doctors you use.

What do you charge Bonita Springs clients?

Nothing. Brokers are compensated by the insurance company you choose, so working with us doesn't raise your premium.
