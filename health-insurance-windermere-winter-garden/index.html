When a spouse works at a large employer: the "family glitch" fix

Many West Orange households have one spouse working for a large employer in hospitality, healthcare or tech. Since 2023, federal rules look at the cost of family coverage, not just employee-only coverage, when deciding whether family members can get Marketplace tax credits. If adding the family to the employer plan is unaffordable under those rules, the spouse and children may qualify for subsidized Marketplace coverage while the employee stays on the work plan. We run the numbers with you.

FAQ

Windermere & Winter Garden health insurance questions

Are AdventHealth and Orlando Health in the same plans?

Not always. Some plans include both systems and some include only one. We check your doctors and preferred hospital against each plan.

I own a restaurant group. Do I need a group plan?

Not necessarily. Many owners compare group, level-funded and ICHRA options. ICHRA reimburses employees for individual coverage and can suit workforces with mixed needs.

My employer plan is affordable for me but expensive for my family. Do we have options?

Possibly. Since 2023, family members may qualify for Marketplace tax credits if family coverage through the employer is considered unaffordable under federal rules. We check your numbers.

Do you help Orlando-area employers?

Yes. We compare small group, level-funded and ICHRA options for businesses across Orange County.

Is there a cost to compare plans with you?

No cost. Carriers compensate brokers, so your premium stays the same.
