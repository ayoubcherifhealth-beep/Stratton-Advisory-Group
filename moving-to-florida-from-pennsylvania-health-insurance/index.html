Relocation guide · Pennsylvania to Florida

Pennsylvania to Florida: your health insurance move, simplified.

Pennie plans, Pennsylvania employer and retiree coverage, and how to land on the right Florida plan without a gap.

Get a Free Quote Call Us Now
Key points
  • Pennie is Pennsylvania's exchange; Florida uses HealthCare.gov.
  • Retiree coverage from a Pennsylvania employer may or may not work here; ask before you move.
  • Pennsylvania has no state coverage requirement, and neither does Florida.
  • Healthy applicants can apply for private Florida plans any month.

Philadelphia suburbs, Pittsburgh and the Lehigh Valley send many retirees and families to Florida's Gulf Coast and Central Florida. Here's how the health insurance side works.

From Pennie to HealthCare.gov

Pennie, Pennsylvania's state-based exchange, enrolls Pennsylvania residents. Florida uses the federal HealthCare.gov platform, so you'll start a new application for your Florida address.

Employer and retiree coverage

Some Pennsylvania employers and unions offer retiree health plans before Medicare. Whether they work in Florida depends on the network. Ask the plan administrator directly, then compare it with a Florida Marketplace plan and a private plan. For working households keeping a Pennsylvania employer, the same question applies to the active plan.

No state penalty on either end

Unlike New Jersey or Massachusetts, Pennsylvania doesn't have a state coverage requirement, and Florida doesn't either. Continuous coverage still matters, because having coverage before your move is what usually unlocks your Florida enrollment window.

Florida choices for Pennsylvania transplants

Most compare a guaranteed-issue Marketplace plan with a private underwritten plan, then check doctors in their new county. If you'll spend part of the year back in Pennsylvania, read our guide for seasonal residents.

Steps for a smooth switch

  1. Ask your employer or retiree plan whether it covers Florida care.
  2. Pick Florida doctors and a pharmacy near your new home.
  3. Enroll within 60 days of moving, or apply for a private plan if you're healthy.
  4. Cancel the Pennsylvania plan effective the day the Florida plan starts.

FAQ

Pennsylvania to Florida: frequently asked

Is Pennie the same as HealthCare.gov?

No. Pennie is Pennsylvania's own exchange. In Florida you'll apply through HealthCare.gov.

Will my Pennsylvania retiree plan cover me in Florida?

It depends on the plan's network. Ask the administrator, and compare it with Florida options before deciding.

Do I need coverage before moving?

Having coverage during the 60 days before your move is usually what qualifies you for a Florida Special Enrollment Period.
