Frequently asked questions

What income is too high for an ACA subsidy in 2027?

For 2027 coverage, premium tax credits generally end at 400% of the federal poverty level. In the 48 contiguous states that is about $62,600 for one person, $84,600 for two and $128,600 for a family of four. Above that, you pay the full premium.

Does earning over $100,000 mean I get no subsidy?

Not always. The cutoff depends on household size. A single person earning $100,000 is above it, but a family of four earning $100,000 is below it and may still qualify for help.

If I don't qualify for a subsidy, what are my options?

You can buy a Marketplace plan at full price, or, if you are healthy, compare private medically underwritten PPO plans. Private plans ask health questions and can decline applicants, so they are not right for everyone.

Is this check a quote or an eligibility decision?

No. It is an educational estimate based on household size and income. Your actual eligibility is decided by HealthCare.gov or your state exchange, and prices depend on your age, location and plan.
