Health Insurance for Florida Small Business Owners

Group plans, level-funded options, ICHRA and QSEHRA explained for owners with 1 to 50 employees, plus what carriers require.

By Ayoub Cherif, licensed health insurance broker (NPN 22089749) · Updated November 8, 2026

Get a Free Quote Call Us Now

Offering health benefits is one of the most effective ways for a small Florida business to hire and keep good people. It's also one of the most confusing purchases an owner makes. Here's how the options work.

Who counts as a small group in Florida

In Florida, small group health insurance generally applies to employers with 1 to 50 employees. Most carriers need at least one enrolled employee who isn't the owner or the owner's spouse. Fully insured small group plans can't turn employees down or charge more because of health history; level-funded plans may ask health questions when pricing the group.

Owners who want a PPO for their family can often get one alongside an HMO for staff, or through individual coverage reimbursed by an ICHRA. See how PPOs work.

QSEHRATax-free reimbursement with annual limits, for employers with fewer than 50 full-time employees and no group plan.Very small teams on a tight budget

What carriers will ask for

  • An employee census: names or initials, dates of birth, ZIP codes and who wants coverage.
  • Participation: a minimum share of eligible employees must enroll or show other coverage.
  • Employer contribution: many carriers require the business to pay a minimum share of employee premiums.
  • Business documents: such as a recent payroll report and proof the business is established.

What it costs, and tax considerations

Group premiums depend on employees' ages, location, plan design and the structure you choose. Premiums the business pays for employees are generally a deductible business expense. Very small employers with lower average wages that buy through the SHOP marketplace may qualify for the Small Business Health Care Tax Credit. Your CPA should confirm what applies.

A simple way to start

  1. Decide on a monthly budget per employee.
  2. Gather a basic census.
  3. Compare a group plan, a level-funded plan and an ICHRA side by side.
  4. Choose, set your contribution, and enroll employees.

FAQ

Quick answers

Can I get group health insurance with just two employees in Florida?

Often, yes. Many carriers offer small group plans starting with one enrolled employee who isn't the owner or the owner's spouse. Requirements vary by carrier.

What's cheaper: a group plan or an ICHRA?

It depends on your team's ages, locations and how much you want to contribute. We price both for your census.
